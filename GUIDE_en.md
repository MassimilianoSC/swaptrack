# SwapTrack guide
App described: commit 7f08154 · Guide text: sha 927c1a427f71 · Date: 06/11/2026

## How to use this guide

1. Find the name of your role below: it is written in the «User menu» (the person icon at the top right), next to your name.
2. Read the part [For everyone](#for-everyone): it applies to every role (sign-in, password, phone, working without network).
3. Then go to the part of your role and look for the question "How do I…?" that you need.
4. The text between «…» is exactly what you see on the screen: buttons, menu items, messages.
5. If a role also does the things of another role, the guide tells you so and sends you to the right section.

## For everyone

SwapTrack follows the **pieces** (the equipment) by reading their barcode. It follows the materials, not the work of people.

### What do you see at the top?

- **The menu of the pages.** On the computer it is the bar on the left (you can narrow it with «Collapse sidebar» and open it again with «Expand sidebar»). On the phone it opens with «Open menu» (the three lines at the top left). At the bottom of the menu, until the app is installed, there is also «Install the app» (see [How do I install the app on my phone?](#how-do-i-install-the-app-on-my-phone)).
- **The bell**: how many open alerts there are (the ASP technician does not see it).
- **The network**: on the computer you see «Online» or «Offline»; on the phone you see only the signal icon, crossed out when there is no network. Next to it you may see «3 waiting to be sent»: these are scans or photos the phone has not sent yet.
- **On the computer, without network**, the strip «You are offline: the data may not be up to date.» appears under the bar: what you see may be old; it goes back to normal when the network is back.
- **The language icon**: «Italiano» or «English».
- **The «User menu»** (person icon): your name and role, «Profile», «Guide», «Sign out».

The «Guide» page of the app holds a few short reminders and, below them, the button «Open the complete guide»: it opens this guide at the part for your role, in a new tab (close it to go back to the app). Once the app has been opened with the network, the guide opens without the network too (on iPhone still to be checked).

### How do I sign in the first time, after the invitation?

Nobody signs up alone: an administrator invites you.

1. Open the link you received by e-mail (or that the administrator gave you).
2. «Set your password» opens. Check that the address shown is yours.
3. Write the password in «New password» and again in «Repeat the password». Rule: «At least 10 characters, with letters and digits.»
4. Press «Save password» once. You see «Password saved. You can now sign in.»
5. Press «Back to sign in» and sign in (see the next question).
6. The first time the app shows you the «Privacy notice»: read it and press «I have read and accept».

If something goes wrong:
- «This link is not valid or has expired. Ask your administrator for a new one, or request a new reset.»: ask the administrator to send you the invitation again.
- The «Save password» button stays off: the password is too short, it has no letters or digits, or the two passwords are different («The two passwords are different.»).

### How do I sign in to the app?

1. Open SwapTrack. «Sign in» opens.
2. Write your «Work e-mail» and the «Password».
3. Press «Sign in». The button shows «Signing in…» until the app opens; if it takes longer you see «It is taking a few more seconds: please wait.».

If your company has turned them on, you also see «Sign in with Google» or «Sign in with Microsoft». If your address already has a password, the app says «This e-mail already has a password: enter it to link the two sign-in methods.»: write the password and press «Sign in». With a Gmail or company Google address, instead, Google links itself to your account and your password stays valid. You can sign in with Google only if the administrator has already invited you with that same address.

Most frequent messages:
- «Wrong e-mail or password.»: check and try again, or use «Forgot your password?».
- «Too many attempts. Wait a few minutes.»: wait and try again.
- «No network.»: the network is needed to sign in the first time.
- «This account is blocked.» or «Access refused: the account is not active.»: talk to your administrator.
- «Account not enabled»: your account has no role; talk to your administrator.

### What do I do if I forgot my password?

1. On the «Sign in» page press «Forgot your password?».
2. Write your «Work e-mail» and press «Send the link».
3. You always see the same message: «If the address belongs to an active account, you will receive an e-mail with a link to reset the password.» This message does not confirm that the e-mail was sent.
4. Open the link in the e-mail: «Choose a new password» opens. Go on as for the first sign-in (steps 3–5).

If the e-mail does not arrive after a few minutes, check the spam folder and then ask the administrator.

### What is the privacy notice and why does it come back?

At the first sign-in, and every time the administrator changes the text, the app shows you the «Privacy notice». Until you press «I have read and accept» you cannot use the pages. If you see «The notice has changed: read it again.», read it again and accept again.

### How do I change the language?

Press the language icon at the top and choose «Italiano» or «English». The choice also applies the next times.

### What is in my profile? How do I change my password?

From the «User menu» press «Profile». You see name, e-mail and role. Name and e-mail cannot be changed here: if they are wrong, ask the administrator.

To change the password:
1. Press «Change password».
2. Write the «Current password» and the «New password» (at least 10 characters, with letters and digits).
3. Confirm. You see «Password changed».

If you see «Sign in again and retry.» or «Wrong password.», sign out, sign in again and retry.

In the profile you also find «Sign out everywhere»: «Ends the sessions on every device. Scans not yet sent on other devices are lost.» If this device still has scans or photos to send, the app does not let you sign out (see the next question).

### How do I sign out? Why does the app sometimes not let me sign out?

1. Open the «User menu» and press «Sign out».
2. If everything has been sent, the app closes and goes back to «Sign in».

If you see «You cannot sign out yet» with «Some scans or photos have not reached the server. Connect to the network and wait until they are sent.», the app is protecting you: the phone still has scans or photos to send («Scans waiting to be sent: 2», «Photos waiting to be sent: 1»). Connect to the network, wait until «waiting to be sent» disappears at the top and try again. The same check applies to «Sign out» on the privacy notice page and to «Sign out everywhere» in the profile.

**Shared phone.** Before handing the phone to a colleague, always sign out with «Sign out» from the «User menu». When another person signs in, the app deletes the data of the previous person from the phone, and the photos of the previous person are never sent in the name of whoever signs in.

If the previous person left scans or photos not yet sent (for example because their session was ended from another phone), whoever signs in sees «Data of the previous user on this device», with how many there are, and two choices:
- «Sign out and let the previous user sign in»: nothing is deleted. On the sign-in page you read «Let the previous user sign in with the network on: their readings and photos will be sent automatically. Then you can sign in.»;
- «Delete their data and continue»: those scans and photos are lost, and you sign in.

When in doubt, choose the first one.

If the app says «The app cannot open» or «Close the other tabs» when it opens, follow the message (close the other windows of the app, turn off private browsing, close and reopen) and press «Try again». If it happens again, call the administrator.

### How do I install the app on my phone?

Installing it is important: this way it opens like an app and works without network too.

**Android**
1. Open the SwapTrack address in the browser.
2. At the top the strip «Install SwapTrack on this device: it opens like an app and works offline too.» appears with «Install the app»: press it and confirm in the browser window. If you do not want to do it now, press «Later»: the strip disappears for 7 days, and «Install the app» stays at the bottom of the menu anyway.
3. Use Chrome. If «Install the app» does not appear, open the Chrome menu (the three dots at the top right) and choose "Install app" or "Add to Home screen" (the names may differ slightly with the phone's language).
4. Open SwapTrack from the icon on the phone, not from the browser.

**iPhone**
1. Open the SwapTrack address with Safari.
2. Tap "Share", then "Add to Home Screen".
3. Open SwapTrack from the icon on the Home Screen, not from Safari.

The «Install the app» item of the menu shows these steps too.

**Computer** (Chrome or Edge): there is no strip. If the browser allows the installation, at the bottom of the bar on the left you find «Install the app».

The app itself reminds you: «Install the app on the Home Screen: tap Share, then "Add to Home Screen". As a Safari tab, iOS can delete scans not yet sent and the offline site list.»

**Updates.** If you see «A new version is available.», press «Reload». If you see «Update required», press the button to reload: the old version no longer works.

### Can I work without network?

Yes, for scanning. This is what happens.

Works without network:
- scanning barcodes and taking photos: they stay on the phone and leave by themselves when the network is back;
- choosing the site or the warehouse from the list already downloaded;
- looking at the data the phone has already downloaded.

Does not work without network (you see «You are offline: this action needs the network.»):
- the first sign-in;
- creating, sending or confirming shipments;
- saving documents, exports, reports, administration changes;
- the automatic reading of documents and labels;
- seeing the history of a piece and the photos already sent.

If you open a page that cannot be shown without network, you see «Not available without network: try again when you are connected.»

**What «waiting to be sent» means.** At the top you see, for example, «4 waiting to be sent»: these are scans and photos made but not yet arrived. When they arrive, the number disappears. Next to each scan you see «Waiting» or «Sent». «Sent» means that the scan has arrived, not that everything is in order: any problems are seen by the office, in the «Anomalies» and «Alerts» pages.

**If a scan is refused.** Rarely the server does not accept a scan: in the list under the camera the row becomes «Refused» and «Reading of … refused by the server» appears, with the reason:
- «It is not allowed for your role or for this place. Check the chosen place and read it again, or tell the office.»
- or «Read it again; if it happens again, tell the office.»

**Before going where there is no network**, open the app while you still have network: it downloads the list of the places and of the pieces. In Home you see «12 available offline · updated …» with the date of the last update.

### What happens if my account is deactivated or blocked?

- If the administrator **deactivates** you, the app shows «Your access has been deactivated» and «Sending your remaining scans… Keep the app open and connected.» Keep the app open with the network: it tries to send what is left, then it clears the phone and takes you back to «Sign in». There you read how it went:
  - «Your access has been deactivated. Your remaining scans have been sent and this device has been cleared.»: everything arrived;
  - «Your access was deactivated and this device was cleared, but some readings or photos were not sent. Tell the office.»: something did not leave; tell the office.

  Scans made without network before the deactivation are still accepted for 7 days.
- If the administrator **blocks you now** (for example for a lost phone), at sign-in you see «This account is blocked.». Scans not yet sent from that phone are lost.
- In both cases, to go back to work ask your administrator to reactivate you.

### How do the tables work?

In almost every office page the data is in tables:
- «Filter» searches the text you see in the rows already shown on that page, not the whole archive;
- tap the title of a column to sort (the order follows the text you see in the column);
- 25 rows per page: use «Previous page» and «Next page»; you see «Rows: …» and «Page 1 of 3».

Places and organizations always appear with their name (for a place: code and name). If you work for an ASP, a place of another ASP appears as «Place of another ASP» and another ASP as «Another ASP».

## ASP technician

**Who you are.** You work at the sites: you remove the old material and install the new one. With the app you scan the code of every piece you remove or install and, at the end, you declare that you have finished at the site.

**What you find in the menu.** «Home» (the choice of the site) and «Guide». At the bottom right there is the big «Scan» button (it does not appear in the scanning screen, where you already are). At the top you see the chosen site: tap it to change it.

**What you do not see.** You see only the sites of your ASP. You do not see alerts, anomalies and the office pages.

### How do I choose the site?

1. Open «Home»: you see «Choose the site».
2. Write part of the code or of the name in the field «Search by code or name».
3. Tap the row of the site. «Selected location» appears.
4. To change it, press «Change location».

The site stays chosen until you close the app. It also works without network, with the list already downloaded. If you see «List not yet updated from the server: this is the copy saved on the phone. It is retried by itself when the network is back.», you can work anyway: the list updates by itself when the network is back.

If something goes wrong:
- «No location available» with «Your organization has no active locations yet. Ask your administrator.»: tell your administrator.
- «The locations could not be loaded.»: press «Try again».
- The site does not appear: maybe it was deactivated or assigned to another ASP. Ask the administrator.

### How do I read a barcode?

You scan in the screen that opens with «Scan». At the top there is the camera; right below there are the controls for every piece: «Undo last», «Photo», the sound, «Burst» and, if the phone has them, «Torch» and «Sun». Further down there is the field of the scanner gun and, if the code cannot be read, the row «Can't read the code?».

**With the camera.**
1. Frame the code inside the colored box.
2. When the reading succeeds, the screen turns green with the code, you hear a sound and the phone vibrates (if it can).
3. The camera stops and «Read the next» appears: press it when you frame the next piece.

**Reading in a row.** If you have to read many pieces one after another, tick «Burst (the camera restarts by itself after every reading)»: after less than a second you can read the next piece without pressing anything. The phone remembers the choice for the next times too.

Helps: «Torch» turns the light on (if the phone has one); the «Sun» slider darkens the image when there is too much light; the speaker icon («Sound on/off») turns the sound off or on.

**With a scanner gun or a ring scanner.** Tap the field «Scanner gun / ring: read here» and scan: the scanner writes the code and sends it by itself.

**If the code cannot be read.** Under the field of the scanner gun you find «Can't read the code?» with two ways:
- **By hand**: write the digits in the field «Type the digits under the barcode» and press «Confirm». It works without network too.
- **«Photograph the label»**: it is there only if the Client administrator has turned on the automatic reading in «Settings» (tab «Features») and if there is network; without network the button does not appear. It is for a damaged label that the camera cannot read.

With «Photograph the label»:
1. Press «Photograph the label» and take a photo of the label. While the app reads it you see «Reading the photo…».
2. «Codes read from the photo» opens. The highlighted characters are uncertain: always check the code on the label and correct it if needed.
3. Press «Confirm» next to the right code. Only then the scan starts, as if you had typed it, and the photo of the label stays linked to the scan. «Cancel» closes without recording anything.

Possible messages (in all these cases type the code by hand):
- «No code was found in the photo: try again or type it by hand.»
- «Some codes were not readable and are missing.»
- «You have reached today's automatic readings.»
- «The automatic reading service is not responding, try again later.»

Good to know:
- If you keep the camera still on the same label, the app does not read it twice and writes «Already read. To read it again, move the phone away for 3 seconds.» This is normal.
- If the app says «This is the product code: read the serial number», you have read the code of the model (the same on all pieces of the same type). Look on the label for the serial number of the single piece and read that one.
- If the piece has two codes and you want to record the product code too, tick «Read product code after the serial»: read the serial number first, the app says «Now read the product code», then read the product code (the camera is ready at once, even if you are not reading in a row). It is always one single piece.

If something goes wrong:
- «Camera not allowed. On iPhone: Settings > Safari > Camera (or the installed app's settings). You can type the code below.»: give the camera permission, or type the code by hand.
- «The reading engine is not available: type the code below.»: use the field to type by hand.

### What happens if you scan a piece again?

When you scan a code, the app checks whether you have already scanned that piece or whether it is already where you are. There are three cases.

1. **You have just scanned it, in the same scanning.** You have already scanned that piece, for the same operation and at the same place, since you opened the screen. A yellow warning appears, for example «… already read a moment ago (at 10:42)». The scan is **not** recorded again and the counter does not go up. It works with the camera, the scanner gun and the digits by hand. If you pressed «Undo last» on that piece, you can scan it again normally.
2. **The piece is already here, in the same state.** For example you record the arrival of a piece that is already recorded as arrived in this warehouse. A yellow warning appears, for example «… has been at MAG-NORD-01 · Magazzino Nord since 04/11/26, 18:10». The scan is sent anyway and nothing changes. In the list the row says «already here» and the counter does not count it as a new piece.
3. **The piece shows a state that does not match.** For example it is already installed at a site, it has already been returned, or it is in another warehouse without a transfer. A **red** warning appears, for example «… shows as “Installed” at SITO-00001 · …: check the label». The scan is sent anyway and the office finds it among the anomalies. Look carefully at the label: it may be on the wrong piece.

Good to know:
- Without network the app uses the data it already has on the phone, which may be old: this is why in cases 2 and 3 the scan always leaves. Only case 1 is certain.
- If the piece belongs to another ASP, the phone does not see it (for confidentiality) and does not warn: the server notices it and opens the anomaly for the office.
- If the piece is at a place the phone does not know, the warning says «another place».
- With a slow network, up to a couple of seconds more may pass before the green flash: the app is checking the piece.
- With a weak signal the app waits for the server at most 2 seconds. If the answer arrives later and the window of the piece is still open, the window updates: in «Sort» the suggested category appears; «Unknown piece» closes by itself if the piece was already known, with the line «Piece recognised: the model is already known.». If you have already chosen, your choice stays.

This behavior is a choice, not a limit: if in your work it must be different (for example blocking case 2 too, or not warning), ask whoever manages SwapTrack.

### How do I record the removal of pieces at a site?

1. Choose the site (see above).
2. Press «Scan».
3. At the top press «Remove» (if it is not highlighted already).
4. Scan one after another the codes of the pieces you remove.

What you see next: under the camera there is the counter, for example «5 readings in this session», and the list of the codes with «Waiting» or «Sent». Further down there is «My scans of today · 12»: the number is how many scans you made today; tap the row to open the list and again to close it.

**If «Unknown piece» appears.** The app says «The code … is new. Pick a model to help the office complete it later, or skip.»
- If you know the model, write part of the name or of the code in «Search model or code» and tap the right result (or choose the model in the «Model» list), then press «Confirm».
- If you do not know it, press «Skip»: the piece is recorded anyway and the office will complete the model. Closing the window with the X also counts as «Skip».
- «No model found: you can skip.»: no model matches what you wrote.

If the piece is among the «Pieces to remove» of the site's BOM and the BOM gives the model, the window does not appear: the model is taken from the BOM.

### How do I record the installation of new pieces?

1. Choose the site.
2. Press «Scan».
3. At the top press «Install».
4. Scan the codes of the new pieces you install.

The new pieces come from the warehouse of your ASP: if a code is not known, the app records it anyway and the office will see it among the anomalies.

### How do I check the pieces expected at the site (the BOM)?

If the list of the expected pieces (the BOM) has been uploaded for the site, in the «Scan» screen, further down after «My scans of today», you see the row «BOM tick list · 3/10» (scanned out of expected): tap it to open the list.
- with «Remove» you see the «Pieces to remove», with «Install» the «Pieces to install»;
- every piece of the list that you scan gets a tick;
- if you scan pieces that are not in the list, they appear below, for example «2 pieces not expected by the BOM»;
- «No lines for this direction.»: the BOM expects no pieces to remove (or to install).

The list counts only your scans of today at that site, made after the BOM was uploaded. The complete check is done by the office.

### How do I undo a wrong scan?

1. Press «Undo last». You see «Last reading undone».
2. You can press it again to undo the scan before, and so on.

Good to know:
- The button is off (with a dashed border) if you have not scanned anything yet in this session.
- The scan does not disappear from the history: in «My scans of today» it stays crossed out with «undone».
- If you undo the only scan of a piece that the app did not know before, the piece also disappears from «Items» and from the counts.
- You can undo only your own scans, at the same place, within 24 hours. Otherwise the office will see «Void not allowed»: ask the office to correct it.

### How do I take a photo of a piece?

1. Scan the piece.
2. Right after, before scanning the next piece, press «Photo» (it is right below the camera) and take the picture.
3. You see «Photo queued for upload»: the photo leaves as soon as there is network.

Good to know:
- The photo is linked to the last scan made in this screen. Before the first scan the «Photo» button is off, with a dashed border, and below it says «Read a piece, then take the photo.»
- After «Undo last» the photo goes to the previous scan; if there are no more, «Photo» turns off.
- «Photos: only the piece, never people». The photo is made smaller and loses the location data before leaving.
- The photo is optional: you can record the piece without it too. The only exception are defective pieces, if the Client administrator has made the photo mandatory (see [How do I sort removed material?](#how-do-i-sort-removed-material)).

If something goes wrong:
- «The photo could not be saved on this device.»: the phone has no space or does not allow it; try again or go on without the photo.
- «2 photos could not be uploaded.»: those photos could not leave. Press «Close» to remove them from the phone (you see «Lost photos removed from this device.»). Tell the office if they were needed.

### How do I declare that I have finished at a site?

1. In the «Scan» screen, with the right site chosen, press «I have finished at this site».
2. You see «Site marked as finished».

Press it only when you have really finished: there is no confirmation question. The site does not close and stays chosen. After this report the office checks the scanned pieces against the expected list of the site (the BOM) and, if something does not match, sees an alert.

### How do I check that my scans have arrived?

- At the top: if «waiting to be sent» is not written, everything has arrived.
- In «My scans of today» every row says «Waiting» or «Sent».
- Before signing out or handing the phone to someone, wait until everything is «Sent» (see "How do I sign out?" in [For everyone](#for-everyone)).

## ASP warehouse

**Who you are.** You work in the warehouse of your ASP. With the app you record the arrivals of material (new and removed), you sort the removed material, you prepare the returns to the client and the transfers between warehouses, and you complete the data of the pieces.

**What you find in the menu.** «Home» (the choice of the warehouse), «Items», «To complete», «Shipments», «Documents», «Alerts», «Anomalies», «Guide». At the bottom right there is the big «Scan» button (it does not appear in the scanning screen, where you already are).

**What you do not see.** You see only the pieces, the documents, the shipments and the alerts of your ASP, plus the pieces that another ASP is transferring to you. In the shipments, the warehouse of another ASP appears as «another organization»; in the other pages a place of another ASP appears as «Place of another ASP».

Scanning the codes (camera, reading in a row, scanner gun, by hand, label photo, «Undo last», «Photo», «Unknown piece») works as for the ASP technician: see [How do I read a barcode?](#how-do-i-read-a-barcode), [How do I record the removal of pieces at a site?](#how-do-i-record-the-removal-of-pieces-at-a-site) (for the «Unknown piece»), [How do I undo a wrong scan?](#how-do-i-undo-a-wrong-scan) and [How do I take a photo of a piece?](#how-do-i-take-a-photo-of-a-piece).

### How do I choose the warehouse?

1. Open «Home»: you see «Choose the warehouse».
2. Search with «Search by code or name» and tap your warehouse.
3. The warehouse stays chosen until you close the app. To change it press «Change location».

Always check the chosen warehouse (at the top) before scanning: every scan is recorded there.

### How do I record the arrival of removed material?

1. Choose the warehouse and press «Scan».
2. Press «Arrival».
3. In the «Material» list leave «Removed material».
4. Leave the category on «Arrival only (sort later)» if you will sort later.
5. Scan the codes of the pieces that arrived.

**If you want to sort in the same pass**, tick «Sort in the same pass» before scanning. For every piece the app uses the suggested category and shows it to you in large letters («Suggested: …»). If the app has no suggestion, you see for example «ABC123: no suggested category, sort later»: the piece is recorded as arrived and you will sort it later. If you want a precise category, choose it in the list before scanning.

If «Unknown piece» appears, choose the model and press «Confirm», or press «Skip».

### How do I prepare a document and attach files or photos?

Documents (DDTs and BOMs) are prepared in «Documents» with «New document». Besides the «Lines» typed by hand, you can attach the real document and let the app read its lines.

**Attaching files or photos.**
1. In the «Document files» box press «Add file or photo» and choose a PDF, photos (JPEG, PNG, WebP), Excel or CSV. If there is nothing yet you see «No files. You can add photos, PDFs or the document's Excel.»
2. The new files show «not saved» until you press «Save». Photos are made smaller before leaving.
3. Tap the name of a file to see it next to the lines; «Open the file» opens it on its own (useful on the iPhone).
4. To take out a saved file press the X next to the name: you see «1 file taken out of this version (it stays in the history).» with «Undo» to change your mind. The file disappears from the new version when you press «Save».

Limits: «At most 10 files per document.»; every file at most 5 MB («File too large: 5 MB at most.»); the new files of one save together at most 6 MB («The new files together exceed 6 MB: save in two steps or use lighter photos.»). Another file type gives «File type not allowed: use PDF, an image (JPEG, PNG, WebP), Excel or CSV.»

**Lines from an Excel or CSV.** When you add an Excel or a CSV, «Excel columns» opens: «Choose which column holds each field. The choice is kept for the next file.» If you see «Loading the saved column choice… The columns you choose stay.», the app is fetching the choice of last time: you can already choose.
1. Choose the column of the «Serial», or those of the «Material code» and of the «Quantity».
2. Below you see how many lines are ready, for example «12 lines ready, 1 not read.»
3. Press «Use these lines». The lines are added to those already typed and you see, for example, «12 lines read from the file (1 not read): check them, then Save.»
4. Check the lines and press «Save».

**Automatic reading.** The «Read automatically» button is there only if the Client administrator has turned on the automatic reading in «Settings» (tab «Features»), and only on a document already saved with at least one photo or one PDF. It needs the network.
1. Add the photos or the PDF of the document and press «Save». If there are files not saved yet you see «Save the new files first, then use the automatic reading.»
2. Press «Read automatically». The proposed lines are added to those already typed: you see for example «8 lines proposed by the automatic reading: check them, then Save.»
3. Always check every line against the paper document, correct what is needed and press «Save». Until you save, nothing is recorded.

Possible warnings: «Some lines were not understood and are missing: compare them with the document.»; «A file cannot be read automatically (for example an Excel): use the column mapping.»; «The document has too many lines: only part of them was read.»; «No line was found in the document.» If you see «You have reached today's automatic readings.» or «The automatic reading service is not responding, try again later.», type the lines by hand or use the Excel.

### How do I record the arrival of new material with the DDT?

First prepare the DDT in the app (once for every DDT):
1. Open «Documents» and press «New document». Otherwise, in the «Scan» screen, in «Arrival» press «Upload DDT»: «New document» opens already set with «DDT (arrival)» and your warehouse.
2. «Kind»: «DDT (arrival)».
3. «Number»: the number written on the paper DDT.
4. «Location»: your warehouse.
5. «Lines»: one serial number per line, copied from the DDT (you can also write "serial material-code": the serial number, a space and the material code). For the pieces without a serial number write a quantity line: "#" followed by the quantity and the material code (for example `#3 MC-A`). You can also attach the DDT and read its lines from an Excel or with the automatic reading: see [How do I prepare a document and attach files or photos?](#how-do-i-prepare-a-document-and-attach-files-or-photos).
6. Press «Save». You see «Saved».

If you see «A document with this number already exists for this ASP.», you have already entered that DDT: check in «Documents» before going on.

Then record the arrival:
1. Choose the warehouse and press «Scan».
2. Press «Arrival».
3. In the «DDT» list choose «DDT …» with the right number: the «Material» list switches by itself to «New material». (If you leave «No DDT (arrival only)» with «New material», the pieces arrive but are not compared with the DDT.)
4. Scan the codes of the pieces.

The pieces that are lines of the chosen DDT do not open the «Unknown piece» window: the model is taken from the DDT. If the DDT does not give the model, the piece goes to «To complete». A piece that is in another open DDT of your warehouse, one you did not choose, does not open the window either, as long as that DDT gives the model.

What you see next: the row «Comparison with DDT … · 3/8» (pieces of the DDT arrived out of expected); tap it to open the box with how many pieces are «arrived», «missing» and «unexpected», and the list of the expected serial numbers with a tick on those scanned. This box counts only **your** scans of today in this warehouse: if a colleague scans with another phone, or if the arrival lasts several days, the complete count is in «Documents» (see below). If you see «The DDT has no serial lines.», the DDT has only quantities and there is no tick list.

Finally, check and close the comparison:
1. Open «Documents» and press «Open» on the DDT.
2. Press «Compare»: you see how many are «Arrived», «Missing», «Unexpected», «Model unknown», under every number the list of the codes and, for the quantities, counted/expected.
3. When the arrival is complete, press «Mark checked». You see «Marked checked».

Good to know: after «Mark checked» the DDT no longer appears in the «DDT» list of the phone. If something does not match, the app creates an alert «DDT (arrival) difference» (see «Alerts»).

### How do I sort removed material?

1. Choose the warehouse and press «Scan».
2. Press «Sort / classify».
3. In the «Category» list leave «Confirm each piece (no fixed category)».
4. Scan a code: the window «Sort …» opens with the code of the piece.
5. If the app has a suggestion, you see «Suggested: …» in large letters: press «Confirm» to use it. Otherwise tap another category. «Defective» and «Leftover» appear only for new material (or for a piece already defective or leftover).

The piece is recorded only when you tap a choice. If the app has no suggestion, the window shows only the categories: tap the right one. If you close the window with «Cancel» or with the X, the piece is not recorded: you see «… not sorted.» and you can scan it again.

**Many identical pieces.** Choose a category in the «Category» list: it becomes fixed and every piece you scan afterwards is recorded with it, without the window, until you change it.

If «installed new material» appears next to the suggested category, that piece had been installed new and then removed.

**Defective or leftover new material.** In the window tap «Defective» or «Leftover». For many pieces in a row, choose «Defective» or «Leftover» in the second list («Removed material (category)»), then scan the pieces. If with this fixed choice you scan a removed piece, the app does not record it and says so in red: «… is removed material: “Defective” and “Leftover” are only for new material. Not recorded: choose a category.»

**Photo of defective pieces.** If the Client administrator has made the photo of defective pieces mandatory, choosing «Defective» in the list you see «A photo is required for defective pieces (setting).», and after every defective piece the app writes «Photo required for the defective piece: take it now.»: press «Photo» next to the message and take the picture.

If something goes wrong:
- You applied the wrong category: press «Undo last» and scan the piece again choosing the right category.

### How do I prepare a return to the client?

1. Open «Shipments» and press «New return».
2. «From»: your warehouse where the return leaves from. «To»: the client warehouse. Press «Create». You see «Shipment created» and the page of the return opens, in the state «Draft».
3. Press «Scan pieces»: scanning opens in the «Return shipment» mode, already in the warehouse the return leaves from (you see it at the top).
4. Scan the codes of the pieces you put in the return. Every piece becomes «In return» and appears among the «Lines» of the return.
5. Go back to the page of the return (from «Shipments», «Open»). Check «Lines» and «Pieces».
6. If you want, keep a copy of the DDT with «Attach DDT file» (PDF or photo). You see «DDT file attached». The file becomes a «DDT (return)» document in «Documents»: after the client's confirmation, its «Compare» compares the pieces of the return with those the client confirmed.
7. Press «Send», write the «DDT number» («Enter the DDT number, then send.») and press «Send». You see «Shipment sent».

Good to know:
- The return is always scanned with «Scan pieces» from the page of the return: «Return shipment» and «Transfer out» appear in the «Scan» screen only when you come to it from a shipment.
- Scanning the same piece twice in the same return does not count it twice.
- To take out a piece scanned by mistake, press «Undo last» at once.

If something goes wrong:
- «Enter the DDT number.»: the number is mandatory to send a return.
- «The shipment is not a draft.»: the return has already been sent or cancelled.
- «File too large: 5 MB at most.» or «File type not allowed: use PDF, an image (JPEG, PNG, WebP), Excel or CSV.»: use a PDF or a smaller photo.
- «Unreadable image: try another photo or a PDF.»

### How do I cancel a shipment draft?

1. Open the shipment in «Shipments».
2. Press «Cancel draft» and confirm («Cancel this draft? Every scan on it will be voided.»).
3. You see «Draft cancelled».

It works only while the shipment is a «Draft».

### How do I transfer pieces to another warehouse of my ASP?

1. Open «Shipments» and press «New transfer».
2. «From»: the warehouse the pieces leave from. «To»: your other warehouse. Press «Create».
3. In the page press «Scan pieces»: scanning opens in the «Transfer out» mode, already in the «From» warehouse. Scan the pieces. Every piece becomes «In transfer».
4. Go back to the page and press «Send» («Send the transfer. The receiving warehouse will record the arrival.»), then «Send». You see «Shipment sent».

Transfers towards another ASP are created only by the Client administrator; you prepare and send them as above, from the draft you find in «Shipments».

### How do I record the arrival of a transfer?

1. Open «Shipments», look for the «Transfer» in the state «Sent» and press «Open».
2. Press «Scan pieces»: scanning opens in «Arrival», already in the receiving warehouse.
3. Scan the pieces that arrived.

What you see next: in the page of the transfer every arrived line shows «Arrived». After the arrival the piece passes to your ASP. When all the pieces have arrived, the transfer becomes «Received» (with the date in the «Summary») and «Scan pieces» disappears. If after some days (7, unless set otherwise) lines are still missing, the alert «Transfer difference» appears to the ASP that sent it.

### How do I complete a piece without a model?

1. Open «To complete»: it is the list of the pieces without a model.
2. Press «Open» on the piece.
3. Press «Edit»: «Edit piece» opens.
4. Choose the «Model». If needed, write the «Condition».
5. Press «Save». You see «Saved».

If you see «Nothing to complete.», there are no pieces without a model.

### How do I search for a piece?

1. Open «Items».
2. Write the **full** code in «Search by code» and press «Search». The page of the piece opens. If you see «No piece has this code.», check the code (part of the code is not enough). Without network the search says instead «Not available without network: try again when you are connected.»: the code cannot be checked until you are back online.
3. Or use the filters «Status», «Site or warehouse» («Type the code or the name and pick it from the list»), «Model». «Clear filters» resets them.

The list shows the most recent pieces, 200 at a time: «Show more» adds another 200.

On the page of the piece you find: «State» (status, location, last scan), «Codes», «Photos», «History» and «Anomalies». In the «History» every scan has date and time, the place, the organization and the role of whoever made it, never the name of the person.

### How do I look at alerts and anomalies?

- **«Alerts»**: the differences to check (DDTs, BOMs, late transfers, material stuck for too many days). One alert per document, shipment or place, with the «Count». The «Object» says what it refers to («Document», «Shipment» or «Place») and the place: tap it to open it. The «Codes» column shows the first 3 codes «Missing», «Unexpected» or with «Model unknown» and how many more there are (for example «and 5 more»). Press «Mark seen» to let others know you have looked at it: it becomes «Seen» for everyone. «Show closed» also shows the closed ones. «No difference now» means that the problem is no longer there today. Alerts do not arrive by e-mail: they stay in the app only.
- **«Anomalies»**: scans that do not match the state of the piece (for example «Piece of another ASP», «Forbidden transition»). From this page you can only look at them and open the piece. To correct them, call your ASP administrator.

## ASP administrator

**Who you are.** You manage your ASP: you invite and manage the technicians and the warehouse staff, you upload the lists of the sites (BOMs), you correct the pieces of your ASP, you look at the numbers and download exports and reports. You can also do everything the ASP warehouse does.

**What you find in the menu.** «Home», «Dashboard», «Items», «To complete», «Shipments», «Documents», «Alerts», «Anomalies», «Exports», «Reports», «People», «Guide». At the bottom right there is the big «Scan» button (it does not appear in the scanning screen, where you already are).

**What you do not see.** Only the data of your ASP. You do not see the other ASPs, nor their pieces (except those that are coming to you).

**From the ASP warehouse:** arrivals, documents and attached files, DDTs, sorting, returns, transfers, pieces to complete, search of pieces, alerts and anomalies work the same way. See the [ASP warehouse part](#asp-warehouse).

On the phone the app has the "field" look (as for the warehouse staff); on the computer it has the office look.

If the Client administrator has turned on the **two-step verification**, at the first sign-in the app asks you to set up an authenticator app on your phone: press «Set up the authenticator app», add the key (or press «Open in the authenticator app»), write the «6-digit code» and press «Confirm»; then sign out and sign in again. If you see «Sign in again to use two-step verification.», sign out and sign in again.

### How do I invite a person?

1. Open «People» and press «Invite user» («The person receives a link to set the password.»).
2. Choose the role: «ASP warehouse» or «ASP technician».
3. Write «First name», «Last name», «Work e-mail» and choose the «Language».
4. Confirm. You see «Invitation created».

What you see next:
- «The invitation e-mail has been sent.»: the person receives the link by e-mail.
- Or «E-mail sending is not configured: copy this link and give it to the person. It is shown only now.»: press «Copy» (it becomes «Copied») and give the link to the person privately. You will not see it again.

If something goes wrong: «An account with this e-mail already exists.»; «This role does not belong to this organization.»; «The organization is not active.» (ask the Client administrator).

### How do I manage the people of my ASP?

In «People», on the row of the person, open «Actions». The menu is there only on the rows with the role «ASP warehouse» or «ASP technician»: it does not appear on the rows of the administrators.

- **«Resend invitation»**: if the person did not receive or lost the link.
- **«Change role»**: choose the new role and press «Save». «The change takes effect at the next token refresh (within 10 minutes).»: that is, within 10 minutes.
- **«Deactivate»**: for someone who leaves the job. «… can no longer use the app. Scans made offline before now are still accepted for 7 days.» The status becomes «Deactivated (grace period)».
- **«Reactivate»**: gives access back to a deactivated person.
- **«Block now»**: only for a lost or stolen phone. «… is blocked immediately on every device. Scans not yet sent are lost.»
- **«Sign out everywhere»**: ends the sessions of the person on every phone. Their scans not yet sent are lost.
- **«Delete»**: closes the account and deletes the personal data. «This cannot be undone.» The history of the pieces stays.

Every action asks for a confirmation. You cannot deactivate, block or delete yourself.

### How do I upload the list of the pieces of a site (BOM)?

1. Open «Documents» and press «New document».
2. «Kind»: «BOM». «Number»: the reference of the list. «Location»: the site.
3. Write the pieces in the two boxes «To remove» and «To install»: one serial number per line (or a quantity line, like `#3 MC-A`).
4. Press «Save».

**From a file.** You can attach the BOM and read its lines (see [How do I prepare a document and attach files or photos?](#how-do-i-prepare-a-document-and-attach-files-or-photos)). For an Excel or a CSV, in «Excel columns» there is also «Remove or install»: choose the column that says it and then, in «Value meaning "remove"» and «Value meaning "install"», the value used in the file; or choose «All to remove» or «All to install». With the automatic reading, the lines whose direction the app does not understand end up in the box «Lines without direction: move them to «To remove» or «To install»», with the warning «Some BOM lines do not say remove or install: move them to the right box.»

**Lines without direction.** A BOM saved in the past can also show the box «Lines without direction: move them to «To remove» or «To install»». Move every line to the right box: while one is left, «Save» answers «Every BOM line must say whether the piece is removed or installed.»

What happens next: when the technician presses «I have finished at this site», and every night, the app compares with the BOM the pieces scanned at the site **after** the BOM was uploaded («Compare» does the same). If something does not match, the alert «BOM difference» appears. If the Client administrator has chosen «Accept silently (no alert)» in «Settings», the pieces scanned at the site but missing from the BOM do not raise the alert.

Every new save of the same document creates a new version (you see it next to the kind, for example «v2»).

### How do I close a document?

1. Open the document from «Documents».
2. Press «Close»: the window «Close the document?» opens. Press «Confirm». You see «Closed».

A closed document can no longer be changed. Closing a document does not close the linked alerts.

### How do I close an alert?

1. In «Alerts», on the row of the alert, press «Close».
2. The window «Close the alert?» opens: write a note if needed («Note (optional):») and press «Confirm». You see «Closed».

Press «Close» only when you have solved it: there is no button to reopen. If the problem comes back, the app reopens the alert by itself.

### How do I correct a piece?

Open the piece (from «Items» or from «Anomalies»). Besides «Edit» (see [How do I complete a piece without a model?](#how-do-i-complete-a-piece-without-a-model)), you have:

- **«Office force»**: choose the right «Status», write the «Reason» (mandatory) and press «Apply correction». Use it when the status of the piece is wrong and a scan is not enough to correct it.
- **«Merge»**: when the same physical piece has been recorded with two codes. Write the «Code of the piece to merge», the «Reason», and press «Merge into this». You can merge only pieces of your ASP. Possible error: «One of these pieces was already merged.»
- **«Correct location»** (the pin icon next to a scan, in the «History»): for a scan recorded at the wrong place. You see the «Previous location»; in «New place» write part of the code or of the name and tap the right place; write the «Reason» and press «Save». You can choose only among the active places you see.
- **«Reveal author»** (icon next to a scan): shows who made that scan, if the data is still available («Author not available.» otherwise). Every request is recorded and you can make at most 20 per hour: use it only when really needed.

All the corrections stay in the history of the piece.

### How do I read the dashboard?

1. Open «Dashboard».
2. You see how many pieces of your ASP are «At ASP warehouse», «Installed», «Removed at site», «Returned», «Sorted», «Defective», «Leftover», «In transfer». Tap a number to open the list of the pieces.
3. Further down: «By flow and status», «By region», «Top models», «By manufacturer», «Sorting outcome» (with the name of every category), «Removed, of which previously new», «Warehouses».

The numbers are updated after the scans and every night: a scan that has just been sent is counted a few seconds later. Reopen the page to see the latest ones.

### How do I download an export?

1. Open «Exports».
2. Choose the filters you need (all optional): «Status», «Model», «Site or warehouse», «Sorting category», «Scanned from», «Scanned until». The dates refer to the last scan of every piece.
3. Press «Create export». You see «Export created» and a new row in the list, with the «Rows» produced.
4. Press «Download» on that row: you download an Excel file. The name of the file contains the date and time of the export.

An export holds at most 10,000 rows. If there are more pieces you see «Export truncated: it holds only the first 10000 rows. Narrow the filters to get everything.» and «truncated» appears on the row: narrow the filters (for example by period or by place) and create several exports.

The list shows only the exports you created. If you see «The data could not be loaded.», try again with the network.

### How do I download a monthly report?

1. Open «Reports».
2. Every month, on day 1, the app prepares by itself the report of the previous month for your ASP.
3. On the row of the month press «PDF» or «XLSX». The name of the file contains the month and the name of your ASP.

## Client warehouse

**Who you are.** You work in the client's warehouse: you receive the returns that arrive from the ASPs and confirm which pieces really arrived.

**What you find in the menu.** «Home», «Items», «Shipments», «Documents», «Alerts», «Guide».

**What you do not see.** You see only the return shipments (not the transfers between ASPs), only the «DDT (return)» documents and only the alerts on the differences of the returns.

### How do I confirm a return?

1. Open «Shipments» and look for the «Return» in the state «Sent». Press «Open».
2. Check the «Summary» (pieces, «DDT number») and the «Lines».
3. Press «Confirm». You read «Untick missing pieces. Add extra pieces by typing their code.»
4. In «Expected pieces» they are all ticked: untick the pieces that did **not** arrive.
5. If pieces arrived that were not in the list, write the code in «Extra pieces» («Type a code and press Enter») and press «Add». To take out a code added by mistake press «×».
6. Press «Confirm». You see «Confirmed».

What you see next: in the page the lines show «Confirmed» or «Missing», and the summary says «Complete: Yes» or «No». If pieces are missing or there are extra ones, the app creates the alert «DDT (return) difference».

**Pieces arrived later.** If a missing piece arrives later, reopen the same return, press «Confirm» again and add it. The pieces already confirmed are not counted twice.

**Piece found in another return.** If a piece marked missing is then confirmed in another return, the row of the first return shows «Confirmed in another return», with the link to that return.

If something goes wrong: «The shipment is not sent.»: the ASP has not sent the return yet, wait. Always check in «Shipments» that the return appears as «Confirmed».

### How do I check the DDT of a return?

1. Open «Documents»: you see only the «DDT (return)». Press «Open» on the document.
2. Press «Compare»: for a DDT attached to a return you see which pieces of the return are «Arrived» (that is, confirmed by you) and which «Missing», with the list of the codes under every number.

### How do I look at and close the alerts on returns?

1. Open «Alerts»: you see the «DDT (return) difference» alerts, with the «Count» of the pieces that do not match and, in the «Codes» column, the first codes.
2. Press «Mark seen» when you have looked at it.
3. When the difference is clear, press «Close», write a note if needed and confirm. Press it only when you are sure: it cannot be reopened by hand.

To see the return the alert refers to, tap the «Object»: the page of the return opens, where you can press «Confirm» to add the pieces that arrived later.

### How do I search for a piece?

1. Open «Items».
2. Write the full code in «Search by code» and press «Search».
3. On the page you see status, location and codes of the piece. Instead of the history you see «The history of the piece is not available for your role.»

**What you see.** The pieces, the shipments, the documents, the alerts and the anomalies of all ASPs. You do not see who made the single scans.

As in [How do I search for a piece?](#how-do-i-search-for-a-piece) of the ASP warehouse. In addition you have the «ASP» filter. The page of the piece is read-only.

2. Press «Compare»: you see how many are «Arrived», «Missing», «Unexpected», «Model unknown», and under every number the list of the codes.

### How do I look at alerts and anomalies?

### How do I download an export?

As in [How do I download an export?](#how-do-i-download-an-export) of the ASP administrator. In addition you can choose the «ASP» filter (empty = all).

### How do I prepare and download a monthly report?

4. Press «PDF» or «XLSX» on the row. The name of the file contains the month and the scope (the client or the name of the ASP).

The report contains the pieces arrived, installed, removed, sorted and returned in the month, the material waiting at the end of the month, the alerts, the main models and sites. It contains no names of people. For the material waiting at the end of the month the report writes when the numbers date from: those taken by the app on day 1 of the following month or, if they are missing, those computed when you generate the report.

## Client administrator

**Who you are.** You manage the whole installation: you create the ASPs, invite the users, prepare sites, warehouses, the catalog of models and the sorting categories, choose the settings. You can correct the pieces of all ASPs, create transfers between ASPs and confirm the returns.

**What you find in the menu.** «Home», «Dashboard», «Items», «To complete», «Shipments», «Documents», «Alerts», «Anomalies», «Exports», «Reports», «Organizations», «Users», «Sites and warehouses», «Catalog of models», «Sorting», «Settings», «Admin log», «Guide».

**Disabling an ASP**: press «Disable» and confirm («Disabling … deactivates all its users.»). «Enable» turns it on again, but its users stay deactivated until you reactivate them one by one from «Users». An ASP cannot be deleted or renamed.

If you see «This is the last active administrator: it cannot be removed or demoted.», first appoint another administrator.

### How do I manage sites and warehouses?

**Creating or changing a location**
1. Open «Sites and warehouses» and press «New location» (or «Edit» on the row).
2. Write «Code» and «Name», choose the «Type» («Site», «ASP warehouse», «Client warehouse») and, for sites and ASP warehouses, the «ASP». Optional: «Address», «Region». Tick «Active».
3. Press «Save». You see «Saved».

Code and type cannot be changed after creation. A location is not deleted: it is deactivated by unticking «Active». Possible error: «This code already exists.».

**Moving a site to another ASP**
1. On the row of the site press «Reassign».
2. Read «Site … and the pieces removed there and not yet collected move to the new ASP.»
3. Choose the new ASP, write a «Reason» if you want, and confirm.

### How do I import sites, warehouses or models from an Excel file?

1. In «Sites and warehouses» (or «Catalog of models») press «Import».
2. «Choose an Excel or CSV file». The first row of the file must contain the titles of the columns. You see «Rows: …».
3. In «Map the columns» say, for every field, the column of the file. Those with the asterisk are mandatory: for locations «Code», «Name», «Type»; for models «Material code», «Model». For the type of the locations the file must use: «Type values: site, asp_warehouse, client_warehouse.» The app remembers the mapping for the next time.
4. Press «Preview»: you see how many rows are «New», «Changed», «Unchanged», «With errors», any «Unknown ASP names» and the list «Row» / «Problems».
5. Press «Import … rows». You see «Import completed». Rows with errors are skipped, the others written.
6. At the end the app tells you if there are active locations or models that were not in the file («… active records are not in the file. Select those to deactivate.»). Tick those to deactivate and press «Deactivate …», or leave everything as it is.

The import never deletes anything and does not create new ASPs: an unknown ASP name is an error (create the ASP first). Frequent row errors: «Code missing.», «Name missing.», «Invalid type.», «Unknown ASP.», «The ASP of a site changes only with Reassign.».

### How do I manage the catalog of models?

1. Open «Catalog of models» and press «New model» (or «Edit»).
2. Write «Material code» (the product code, the same on every piece of that model) and «Model». Optional: «Manufacturer», «Equipment type».
3. Press «Save».

The material code cannot be changed after creation. A model is not deleted: it is deactivated.

### How do I prepare sorting categories and rules?

**Categories**
1. Open «Sorting» and press «New category» (or «Edit» on the row of a category: «Edit category» opens).
2. Write «Label (Italian)» and «Label (English)» (both mandatory) and an «Order» (a number from 0 to 999: it decides the position in the list). Tick «Active».
3. Press «Save».

**Rules** (they are used to suggest the category to the warehouse)
1. Press «New rule» (at least one category is needed), or «Edit» on the row of a rule: «Edit rule» opens.
2. Give at least one of «Site code (optional)», «Model», «Equipment type», and choose the «Category».
3. Press «Save».

When several rules apply to the same piece: «Most specific wins: site + model > site + equipment type > model > equipment type > site.» Categories and rules are not deleted: they are deactivated. Possible errors: «An active rule with the same keys already exists.», «A rule needs a site, a model or an equipment type.».

### How do I create a transfer between two ASPs?

1. Open «Shipments» and press «New transfer».
2. «From»: the warehouse of the ASP that sends. «To»: a warehouse of **another** ASP (the list shows only those).
3. Press «Create». You see «Shipment created».

Then the warehouse of the sending ASP finds the draft in «Shipments», scans the pieces and sends it; the receiving ASP records the arrival (see [How do I record the arrival of a transfer?](#how-do-i-record-the-arrival-of-a-transfer)). For transfers inside the same ASP you are not needed: the warehouse staff create them. You can cancel a draft with «Cancel draft».

### How do I accept a piece that arrived at another ASP?

If an ASP scans a piece that belongs to another ASP without a transfer, the anomaly «Piece of another ASP» is raised.
1. Open the piece (from «Anomalies», «Items» column).
2. In the «Anomalies» section press «Accept move» («Writes an office correction one instant before the scanning arrival; the piece becomes owned by the scanning ASP.»).
3. Write a reason if you want and press «Confirm».

### How do I use the settings?

Open «Settings». Every tab has its own «Save»: changing tab does not save. After saving you see «Saved».

- **«Branding»**: «App name», «Primary color», «Logo» (PNG, JPEG, SVG or WebP, at most 500 KB; it also appears on the sign-in page). «Remove logo» removes it.
- **«Sign-in»**: «Google sign-in», «Microsoft sign-in (company account)», «Two-step verification for administrators», «Sign out desktop sessions after inactivity» with the «Inactivity minutes» (from 5 to 480; it applies only to computers, and it does not sign out if there are scans waiting to be sent). Google, Microsoft and the two-step verification need a technical preparation of the sign-in service: turn them on only after talking with whoever installed SwapTrack.
- **«App»**: «Minimum version» (empty = no block; if you write it, whoever has an older version must update). «Invitations are sent by e-mail (Firebase Authentication)»: tick it to send invitations by e-mail; if it is off, or if the e-mail does not leave, the invitation shows you the link to copy. «Forgot your password?» is always sent by e-mail.
- **«Privacy»**: «Notice text (Italian)» and «Notice text (English)». Changing a text, everybody will have to accept it again. «Ask everybody to accept again (new version)» asks for it even without changing the text. «Remove the author from old events» with «After how many days» (from 30 to 3650): removes from old scans the record of who made them.
- **«Thresholds»**: «Days before stale_material» (after how many days stuck material raises the alert «Stale material»; default 30) and «Days before transfer_difference» (after how many days an incomplete transfer raises «Transfer difference»; default 7).
- **«Photos»**: «Photo mandatory for defective pieces». If you tick it, when a warehouse worker sorts a piece as «Defective» the app asks for the photo at once (see [How do I sort removed material?](#how-do-i-sort-removed-material)).
- **«Field rules»**: «A piece found at a site but not in the BOM». Choose «Accept and create an alert» (the starting choice) or «Accept silently (no alert)». In both cases the scan is accepted; only whether the «BOM difference» alert is raised for those pieces changes.
- **«Features»**: «Automatic reading of documents and damaged label codes (AI)». «Proposes the lines of DDTs and BOMs by reading photos and PDFs, and the codes of damaged labels from their photo. Lines and codes are saved only after the check of whoever uploads the document or scans the piece.» If you turn it on, «Read automatically» appears in the documents and «Photograph the label» in the «Scan» screen; they need the network. Every person can make at most 20 automatic readings a day (documents and labels together); this number cannot be changed from this page.

### How do I read the admin log?

Open «Admin log»: for every management action you see «When», «Action», «By», «Target», «Details». Besides the management of users, ASPs, locations, models and settings, you also find «Document saved», «Pieces merged», «Location of a reading corrected» and «Anomaly resolved». The log cannot be changed or deleted. It does not record the scans of technicians and warehouse staff.

## Words you meet

- **Piece**: a single physical piece of equipment, recognized by its serial number.
- **Serial number (serial)**: the unique code of the single piece. It is the one to scan.
- **Product code / material code**: the code of the model, the same on all pieces of the same type. It does not identify a piece.
- **Model**: the type of equipment (from the «Catalog of models»).
- **Site**: the place where pieces are removed and installed. Every site belongs to one ASP only.
- **ASP warehouse**: the warehouse of an installer company. It is also the name of the role of the warehouse staff.
- **Client warehouse**: the warehouse where the returns arrive. It is also the name of a role.
- **ASP**: an installer company. Each one sees only its own data.
- **New material**: new pieces that go from the client to the ASP warehouse and then to the site.
- **Removed material**: pieces taken away from the sites, which go back to the ASP warehouse, are sorted and returned to the client.
- **DDT**: the transport document. «DDT (arrival)» for new material that arrives; «DDT (return)» for a return to the client.
- **BOM**: the list of the pieces expected at a site, divided into «To remove» and «To install».
- **Sorting**: giving every removed piece a category.
- **Category**: the group a removed piece ends up in after sorting (decided by the Client administrator).
- **Defective / Leftover**: new material that is not installed because it is broken or extra.
- **Return**: a shipment of pieces from the ASP warehouse to the client warehouse; also the final state of the returned piece.
- **Transfer**: a shipment of pieces between two ASP warehouses (of the same ASP or of two different ASPs).
- **Draft**: a shipment being prepared, not sent yet.
- **Alert**: a difference to look at (on a document, a shipment or a place). It stays in the app only.
- **Anomaly**: a scan that does not match the history of the piece. The office sees it.
- **Scan**: the record of a code that was read (with the camera, a scanner gun, by hand or from the label photo).
- **Burst**: scanning one piece after another without pressing anything between one scan and the next. It is turned on with «Burst (the camera restarts by itself after every reading)»; without it, after every scan press «Read the next».
- **Automatic reading**: the app proposes the lines of a document or the code of a damaged label by reading a photo or a PDF. It is there only if the Client administrator has turned it on; what it proposes must always be checked and confirmed.
- **«Waiting»**: scans or photos made on the phone but not arrived yet. They leave by themselves when there is network.
- **«Sent»**: the scan has arrived. It does not mean that everything is in order.
- **«To complete»**: pieces without a model.
- **Office force**: a correction of the state of a piece made by an administrator, with a reason.
- **Deactivate / Block**: taking access away from a person; blocking is immediate and loses the scans not sent.
